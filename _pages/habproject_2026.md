# Microbial-Enabled Mars Colonization Project

The MM&H program is inviting proposals for the Microbial-Enabled Mars Colonization Project. The detailed Request for Proposals is provided below. The deadline for submission is **10 January 2027 at 18:00 UTC**.

## Request for Proposals (RFP)

### Art. 1 – General

The MM&H program supports innovative solutions for long-term human colonization of the cosmos. This RFP requests proposals for the establishment of a **permanent colony of 5,000 settlers on Mars**, capable of operating for **ten years without resupply from Earth**.

The primary objective is to develop an integrated colonization strategy in which microbial processes provide essential functions for human habitability. A combination of microbial, physical, chemical and other biological technologies is permitted, but **microbial solutions must be the main focus of the project**.

Proposals must address both the establishment of the colony and its subsequent operation. Initial supplies and equipment imported from Earth must be explicitly identified and distinguished from resources obtained or regenerated on Mars.

### Art. 2 – Proponents

Proposals may be submitted by class participants organized into teams of **a minimum of two and a maximum of three people**. Each team must identify a Principal Investigator responsible for submission.

### Art. 3 – Colonization target and minimum requirements

The project must focus exclusively on Mars. Teams must select and justify a location and describe the configuration of the colony.

Each proposal must address, at minimum, the following five areas:

1. **Habitat and atmosphere.** Colony configuration, internal environmental conditions, initial atmosphere provision, oxygen regeneration and carbon dioxide removal.

2. **Water supply and recycling.** Acquisition of local water, treatment and supply, and recovery from domestic, agricultural and industrial use.

3. **Food production.** Production of a nutritionally adequate food supply through microbial technologies and complementary approaches.

4. **Waste processing and nutrient cycling.** Processing of human waste and residual biomass, recovery of essential nutrients and their integration into colony production systems.

5. **Local resources and biomanufacturing.** Acquisition and processing of local resources, including microbial-assisted extraction, and production of biomaterials or other essential supplies.

These five areas are **minimum requirements**. Teams may include additional systems or functions that strengthen their colonization strategy. Energy sources and requirements must be addressed throughout the proposal.

### Art. 4 – Proposal format

The proposal must not exceed **five pages**, excluding the cover sheet and references. Use Arial, 11-point font, single line spacing and 2 cm margins on all sides. All figures, tables, calculations and the timeline must be included within the five-page limit.

The proposal must contain:

1. **Scientific background and rationale.** A focused account of the scientific principles and available evidence supporting the proposed strategy.

2. **Design and microbial approaches.** Description of the colony and its essential systems, identifying the microorganisms or consortia, metabolic processes, operating conditions and complementary technologies involved.

3. **Quantitative support.** Calculations demonstrating whether the proposed microbial systems can support the colony at the required scale, as specified in Art. 5.

4. **Integrated flow diagram.** A clearly labelled diagram showing the connections between the proposed systems, including major material inputs, transformations, outputs, recycling pathways and losses. The diagram must distinguish microbial processes from complementary technologies and identify inputs from initial supplies and local Martian resources.

5. **Timeline and overall feasibility.** A timeline covering preparation, establishment, biological system development and full operation, together with the main scientific and technical limitations of the proposed strategy.

### Art. 5 – Microbial processes and quantitative requirements

Proposals must demonstrate how microbial metabolism supports the operation of the colony. Listing potentially useful microorganisms or technologies without explaining their function, scale and integration will not satisfy this requirement.

Teams must provide order-of-magnitude calculations addressing, at minimum:

- **Oxygen and carbon dioxide:** compare settlers’ oxygen requirements and carbon dioxide production with the capacity of the proposed photosynthetic systems, accounting for major additional biological oxygen demands.
- **Food:** compare settlers’ dietary energy and protein requirements with the productivity and nutritional contribution of the proposed microbial food systems and complementary food sources.
- **Water:** relate water demand and wastewater generation to the treatment capacity and recovery performance of the proposed microbial systems and associated technologies.
- **Waste and nutrients:** estimate the capacity of microbial processes to transform major waste streams and recover nutrients for reuse, identifying losses and required replacement inputs.
- **Energy and cultivation scale:** estimate the energy and substrate requirements of the principal microbial processes and the cultivation area, reactor volume or processing capacity needed.

Calculations must state assumptions, provide units and cite the sources of the values used. They must connect colony requirements to microbial rates, yields or conversion efficiencies and account for the contribution of complementary technologies. Teams must avoid counting the same resource or production output more than once across interconnected systems.

### Art. 6 – Poster and project submission

Each team must submit:

1. **One proposal PDF**, containing the completed cover sheet, the proposal and references. The cover sheet must identify all team members and the Principal Investigator and include their signatures.
2. **One high-quality A0 landscape poster PDF**, presenting the colony concept, principal microbial technologies, integrated system design and key quantitative results.
3. **The proposal has to be submitted exclusively through the online portal** available at [https://forms.gle/k2G1Av7ECfmCQKbz6](https://forms.gle/k2G1Av7ECfmCQKbz6)

Files must use the following naming convention:

- `SurnamePI-Short_project_title-2027-proposal.pdf`
- `SurnamePI-Short_project_title-2027-poster.pdf`

The submission deadline is **10 January 2027 at 18:00 UTC**. Proposals and posters must be submitted through the designated online submission system. Only submissions complete in all their parts will be evaluated.

### Art. 7 – Evaluation criteria

Proposals will be evaluated according to the following criteria:

1. **Scientific and technical merit**, including the rationale for the selected microbial processes — maximum 25 points.
2. **Innovation and central role of microbial solutions** — maximum 20 points.
3. **Quantitative feasibility and adherence to the RFP**, including support for 5,000 settlers and ten years without resupply — maximum 30 points.
4. **Overall strategy and integration**, including the flow diagram, timeline and clarity of the proposal and poster — maximum 25 points.
